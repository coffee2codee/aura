# ✦ Aura Estate

### *Where exceptional spaces find exceptional people.*

A modern real-estate experience designed around the idea that finding a home should feel as beautiful as the home itself.

Aura Estate is a visual-first real-estate website featuring immersive scroll interactions, elegant property discovery, and an AI-powered assistant - all wrapped in a clean, premium interface.

## 🏛️ The Experience

**Immersive from the first scroll.**

The hero section uses a scroll-driven visual sequence to create a cinematic introduction instead of a traditional static landing page.

From there, the experience flows through curated properties, locations, details, and enquiries while keeping the visual language consistent throughout.

## ✨ Highlights

**🎞️ Cinematic Hero**  
A scroll-based visual experience that responds directly to the user's movement.

**🏡 Curated Properties**  
Explore premium homes with details including location, price, area, bedrooms, amenities, and availability.

**⌕ Smart Property Discovery**  
Filter properties based on what you're looking for and explore the ones that fit.

**✦ AI Real-Estate Assistant**  
An interactive assistant built to help with property discovery, buying, renting, EMI, RERA, documentation, amenities, and site visits.

**♡ Seamless Enquiries**  
A simple way for interested buyers to enquire about a property.

**↗ Responsive Experience**  
Designed to feel polished across desktop, tablet, and mobile.

**◌ Thoughtful Motion**  
Animations and transitions bring the interface to life while respecting reduced-motion preferences.

## 🛠️ Built With

`React` · `Vite` · `Tailwind CSS` · `JavaScript` · `React Router`

## 🎨 The Visual Direction

Aura Estate takes inspiration from modern luxury real-estate experiences — large imagery, spacious layouts, muted tones, elegant typography, and subtle movement.

The goal wasn't to make every section compete for attention, but to create a calm, premium experience where the properties remain the focus.

## 🤖 AI Assistant

The built-in assistant is focused specifically on real-estate conversations.

It can help users with:

- Finding suitable properties
- Buying, selling & renting
- Home loans & EMI
- RERA and documentation
- Locations & amenities
- Property enquiries
- Site visits

The assistant also includes a local fallback, allowing the experience to continue even when an external AI service isn't available.
